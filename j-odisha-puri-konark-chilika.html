The journey

Odisha is the best-kept secret in Indian travel largely because nobody organises it well. The temples rival Tamil Nadu’s, the coast is emptier than Goa’s was thirty years ago, and the state gets a fraction of the visitors. The usual mistake is treating it as a Puri pilgrimage with a Konark photo stop bolted on. That wastes the place.

We build the trip around unhurried mornings. Darshan at the Jagannath temple is arranged early, with our escort managing the queue and the practicalities so that non-Hindu members of your party know exactly what they can and cannot enter, honestly and in advance. Konark we do late afternoon, when the sun comes low across the carved wheels and the tour buses have gone. Chilika is a dawn boat, Irrawaddy dolphins are genuinely likely at Satapada, though we will never promise wildlife, and Raghurajpur is a working village of pattachitra painters where you sit on a veranda and watch a canvas take shape, not a souvenir stop.

This is one of our most senior-friendly journeys: short drives, flat terrain, and a coast where the schedule breathes. It lands hardest for travellers who did the golden triangle years ago and want India to surprise them again.

Day by day

Six days, start to finish. The route, then the rhythm of it.

Day 1Bhubaneswararrival & old temples
Days 2–5Puridarshan, Konark & Chilika
Day 6Bhubaneswardepart
  • Day 1

    Arrive Bhubaneswar, short temple walk around Mukteshvara and Rajarani if energy allows, otherwise straight to the hotel. Easy first evening.

  • Day 2

    Bhubaneswar’s old town properly, Lingaraj from the viewing platform, then the Udayagiri caves. Afternoon drive to Puri (1.5 hrs). Sunset on the beach.

  • Day 3

    Early Jagannath darshan with our escort handling logistics. Afternoon rest, then Raghurajpur, pattachitra painters and Gotipua dance in the village where it was kept alive.

  • Day 4

    Slow morning. Late-afternoon Konark, timed for low light on the stone; the interpretation walk is our escort’s favourite hour of this trip. Evening free in Puri.

  • Day 5

    Dawn drive to Satapada for the Chilika lagoon boat, dolphins likely, birdlife certain in winter. Seafood lunch by the water. Return to Puri.

  • Day 6

    Drive to Bhubaneswar airport (2 hrs) for departure.

Where you stay

One night in Bhubaneswar near the old temple quarter, then four nights in Puri at a sea-facing property we have used for years, chosen for its quiet stretch of beach, its kitchen, and staff who remember guests. Nothing flashy anywhere on this route; comfort and location are the standard.

Who it’s for

Pilgrims who want darshan handled with dignity rather than hustle, seniors, and anyone drawn to temples, painters and empty beaches. Honest note: non-Hindus cannot enter the Jagannath temple’s inner precinct, we say so upfront and make the rest of the day worth it. Not for resort-luxury seekers or nightlife.
