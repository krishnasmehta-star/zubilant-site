About

Seventeen years of getting people to the right place, in the right order.

Zubilant is a division of Stuti Tourism Pvt. Ltd., founded in 2009 and repositioning from tour operator to designer of curated travel.

§ 01
Our philosophy
Travel designed around experiences, not packages. Two lines: India, and the world.

We design the order, not just the list.

Most itineraries are a list of places. Ours are an order — forts before the stone heats up, the houseboat at the end and not the start, two days doing nothing at 3,500 metres before anyone climbs higher.

That ordering is the seventeen years. It is not something you can put in a brochure, and it is the entire difference between a trip a family remembers and one it endures.

We print the drive lengths, the maximum altitude and the nearest hospital on every journey. Nobody else does, because those facts lose sales. They also prevent the one bad trip that costs you a family for a decade.

§ 02
The founder
Use the UN/CEFACT wording exactly. Never “works with the United Nations”.

Sachin Mehta

Founder and CEO. Zubilant’s founder serves as Travel and Tourism Domain Coordinator at UN/CEFACT, the United Nations body that sets global standards for travel and trade data.

That role is the strongest credibility asset the company has, and it is why the operational detail on these pages is not marketing — the standards work and the itinerary work come from the same habit of mind.

The founder’s note in his own words, his signature, and the countries operated in with rough years are with Sachin for sign-off. The current site states “over 27 years of expertise”; the confirmed facts say the company was founded in 2009. Both cannot be on the same page — see the build notes.
