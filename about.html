A note from the founder

Why Zubilant exists, in my own words.

Namaste. I am Sachin Mehta. I started Stuti Tourism in 2009 because I believed travel in India could be done with more care than the industry was giving it. Seventeen years and more than 800,000 travellers later, that belief has not changed. The company has grown. The idea has not.

Here is the idea. Most itineraries are a list of places. Ours are an order. Forts before the stone heats up, the houseboat at the end and not the start, a quiet day at altitude before anyone climbs higher. The list is easy. The order is the seventeen years.

We also tell you the things most brochures leave out: the drive lengths, the maximum altitude, the nearest hospital. Facts like that can lose a sale. We print them anyway, because they prevent the one bad trip, and because you cannot build trust on a page that only says pleasant things.

We are an Indian company in the oldest sense. Atithi Devo Bhava, the guest is God, is not a slogan here; it is the standard we hold every hotel, driver and guide to. And my ambition has not shrunk with the years. I want India to stand among the three most-loved destinations on earth, and I want us to have played our part in that.

If you are planning something, a family holiday, a pilgrimage, your parents’ first flight, call us. You will speak to a person who designs journeys, and the conversation costs nothing.

Sachin Mehta Founder & CEO, Zubilant · Stuti Tourism Pvt. Ltd.
What we hold ourselves to

Six things we refuse to compromise.

Every company has values on a wall somewhere. These are the ones we can be held to.

The guest is God

Atithi Devo Bhava is the brief on every journey. It is the standard we hold every hotel, driver and guide to, and ourselves first.

Honest facts, printed

Drive lengths, maximum altitude, the nearest hospital. If a fact matters to your safety or comfort, it is on the page, even when it costs us a sale.

Detail is the product

Room splits, dietary counts, the wet-weather plan. Detail is not a garnish on the journey; it is the journey working.

One person, start to finish

You deal with a name, not a helpline. The person who designs your journey is the person who answers when you call.

Your details stay with us

A phone number given to plan a journey is used to plan that journey. Never sold, never passed to third parties, never added to a spam list.

No surprise charges

The quote lists what is included, line by line. If something changes, you hear it from us in writing before it costs you anything.
