Kazakhstan · 6 nights · Moderate

Almaty & the Kazakh Mountains

Six nights out of Almaty to Charyn Canyon, the Kolsai lakes and Big Almaty Lake, Central Asia's most underrated mountain country, visa-free for Indians and four hours from Delhi.

The journey

Here is the arithmetic nobody runs: Almaty is closer to Delhi than Bangkok is, Kazakhstan is visa-free for Indian passport holders, and the mountains start where the city's last street ends. Yet for every hundred Indians in Baku there is perhaps one in Almaty. That imbalance will not survive 2027, which is exactly why we built this journey now.

The shape of the trip is a city with mountains bolted on. Almaty itself earns a day, the timber Zenkov Cathedral, the Green Bazaar where the horse-milk sellers will insist you try, and the Medeu cable car rising to Shymbulak at 3,200 metres before lunch. Then east: Charyn Canyon, a red-rock gorge that photographs like Arizona and costs like a state bus ticket, and two nights near the Kolsai lakes, where spruce forest drops to alpine water and the second lake rewards those willing to walk three hours for it. We finish at Big Almaty Lake, an implausible turquoise at 2,500 metres, forty minutes from your last supper in the city.

This is for groups of friends and younger travellers who want the mountains without the paperwork, no visa, no permits, no altitude drama. Rising for 2027; the smart money goes before the crowds do.

Day by day

  • Day 1

    Arrive Almaty, transfer, easy afternoon, Panfilov Park and the Zenkov Cathedral on foot, dinner of plov and fresh bread. Gentle start, on purpose.

  • Day 2

    Green Bazaar in the morning, Medeu and the Shymbulak cable car before the afternoon haze; evening free in the café quarter.

  • Day 3

    Drive east to Charyn Canyon (about 4 hours), the Valley of Castles walk in the late-afternoon light when the rock goes copper; on to the Saty valley for the night.

  • Day 4

    Kolsai Lake One in the morning; the three-hour trail to Lake Two for the willing, horseback for the tempted, lakeside idleness for the wise.

  • Day 5

    Kaindy Lake and its drowned spruce forest by 4x4 in the morning, drive back to Almaty, farewell dinner.

  • Day 6

    Big Almaty Lake excursion in the morning, afternoon free for the bazaar's dried-fruit stalls and last-minute chocolate.

  • Day 7

    Transfer to Almaty airport, fly home.

Where you stay

A comfortable, central hotel in Almaty with the mountains visible from the breakfast room. In the Saty valley, a family-run guesthouse, plain rooms, enormous dinners, hosts who have been feeding our groups since we first drove this road. Character and warmth over stars; that is the honest deal in rural Kazakhstan.

Who it’s for

Friends' groups, young couples and solo-friendly travellers who like a trip with dust on it. The Kolsai walk is optional but the trip is built for people who will take it. Not for travellers wanting luxury consistency, the valley guesthouse is charming, not plush, and not for anyone who cannot manage vehicle time on rough final stretches.
