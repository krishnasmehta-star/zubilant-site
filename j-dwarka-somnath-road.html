Gujarat · 5 nights · Gentle

Dwarka & Somnath by Road

Five nights along Gujarat's temple coast — Dwarkadhish and Somnath with darshan arranged and queues managed, Beyt Dwarka by boat, and the Gir lions in between.

The journey

Two of the four dhams sit on one coastline, joined now by roads good enough to make the journey part of the pilgrimage rather than the price of it. We built this journey for the adult children who call us saying the same thing: my parents want to go, and I want them looked after.

So it is looked after. Darshan slots arranged in advance at both temples, wheelchairs organised where needed, hotels chosen for lifts and bland-food kitchens that understand a fast, drives capped at four hours with proper tea stops. The evening aarti at Somnath, with the sea behind the temple, is worth the whole trip and we schedule it twice in case weather or crowds interfere with the first.

Between the two temples, Gir — because Asiatic lions at dawn are their own kind of darshan.

Day by day

  • Day 1

    Arrive Rajkot or Jamnagar, drive to Dwarka. Evening aarti at Dwarkadhish.

  • Day 2

    Morning darshan managed properly, then Beyt Dwarka by boat and Nageshwar Jyotirlinga.

  • Day 3

    Rukmini temple, then the coast road to Gir. An easy evening.

  • Day 4

    Dawn safari at Gir. Rest, then on to Somnath by late afternoon; first evening aarti.

  • Day 5

    Somnath darshan at opening, Bhalka Tirth, and the second aarti by the sea.

  • Day 6

    Return to Rajkot, depart.

Where you stay

The best available hotels at Dwarka and Somnath — chosen for lifts, proximity to the temples and kitchens that respect a vrat — and a comfortable lodge at Gir.

Who it’s for

Senior travellers and the families accompanying them. Drives are short, queues are managed, and wheelchairs can be arranged at both temples with notice. Built with Senior Travel Saathi standards throughout.
