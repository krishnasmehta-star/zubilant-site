Mauritius · 6 nights · Gentle

Mauritius, Unhurried

Six nights across the island's north and south, Chamarel's coloured earth, a catamaran day on real water, and enough unscheduled beach to remember why you came. Visa-free for Indians.

The journey

The standard Mauritius package is a resort with an island attached: seven nights behind one gate, a buffet you could be eating in any country, and a single rushed "south tour" on day five. We think the island deserves better, and so do you, Mauritius is small enough that nowhere is more than two hours from anywhere, which makes it perfect for a trip that moves twice and hurries never.

Three nights in the north put you near Grand Baie and Cap Malheureux for the postcard church and the market at Port Louis, where a third of the island's story is Indian and the dholl puri proves it. Then three nights on the wilder south coast: the coloured earths of Chamarel, where seven shades of volcanic dune sit in one small field, the gorge viewpoints of Black River, and a full catamaran day, grilled fish on board, snorkelling off the stern, and the kind of blue that does not need a filter. Entry is visa-free for Indian passport holders; you clear immigration with a stamp and a smile.

This is a honeymoon island that also, quietly, works beautifully for three generations at once, flat, safe, short drives, and sea that stays shallow a long way out. May to December is the honest season: cooler, drier, and outside cyclone risk.

Day by day

  • Day 1

    Arrive Mauritius, transfer north, feet in the water before sunset, nothing else planned, on purpose.

  • Day 2

    Port Louis morning, the central market, Aapravasi Ghat and the waterfront; afternoon back on the beach.

  • Day 3

    Cap Malheureux and Grand Baie at an ambling pace; optional undersea walk or glass-bottom boat; seafood dinner.

  • Day 4

    Transfer south via the Bois Chéri tea estate; Chamarel's seven-coloured earths and the rum distillery in the afternoon.

  • Day 5

    Full catamaran day, sail, snorkel, grilled lunch on board; the day everyone names afterwards.

  • Day 6

    Black River Gorges viewpoints and Grand Bassin's temple lake in the morning, final beach afternoon, farewell dinner.

  • Day 7

    Transfer to the airport, fly home.

Where you stay

A beachfront property in the north with rooms that open toward the water, and a quieter resort on the south coast where the reef does the entertaining. Both are places we have used for years, chosen for their stretch of sand and their kitchens rather than their brochure adjectives. Honeymoon touches arranged where they apply, tell us.

Who it’s for

Honeymooners, and multi-generational families celebrating something, the island handles both in the same week without strain, and the pace suits travellers of any age. Senior-friendly throughout: short flat walks, short drives, lifts or ground-floor rooms everywhere. Not for adventure-hunters or nightlife-seekers; Mauritius evenings are long dinners, not late clubs.
