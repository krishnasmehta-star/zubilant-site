§ 04
Before you enquire
Saying who we are wrong for is the fastest way to be believed about who we are right for.

Who this isn’t for.

We would rather lose the enquiry than take a group that will not enjoy the trip.

Not for

Lowest-price shopping

We are not the cheapest, and an itinerary that competes on price competes by removing the parts that cost money — the escort, the hotel with the lift, the private vehicle.

Not for

Last-minute departures

Permits, darshan slots and the properties we actually rate need lead time. A trip arranged in four days is somebody else’s trip.

Not for

Party travel

Nightlife is not what we design for. If that is the holiday, there are people who do it better than us.
