Kerala · 7 nights · Gentle

Kerala Backwaters, Slowly

Seven unhurried nights from Kochi through Alleppey to Kumarakom and the hills, a houseboat, a spice garden, and afternoons with nothing in them, on purpose.

The journey

Kerala punishes hurry. The state's whole argument is the pace of water through a paddy field, and an itinerary that does six towns in seven days has missed the point before it starts.

So we do less. Kochi properly, not quickly, the fish nets at sunset, a Kathakali evening where someone explains what the eyebrows mean. One full night and a full day on a houseboat, because the backwaters at six in the morning are the best thing in South India and day-trippers never see them. Then Kumarakom, where the afternoon programme is a chair facing the lake.

Grandparents manage this trip beautifully. So do six-year-olds. That is not an accident.

Day by day

  • Day 1

    Arrive Kochi. Fort Kochi walk in the evening light, dinner by the water.

  • Day 2

    Mattancherry, the synagogue, spice warehouses. Kathakali in the evening, explained properly.

  • Day 3

    Drive to Alleppey, board the houseboat by noon. Lunch on board is the best meal of the week.

  • Day 4

    Morning on the water, then Kumarakom. The afternoon is yours and the lake's.

  • Day 5

    Village walk with a local naturalist, toddy tappers, coir weavers, a temple tank.

  • Day 6

    To Thekkady through rubber and cardamom country. Spice garden with the family that owns it.

  • Day 7

    Periyar lake in the morning, an Ayurvedic massage for those who want one.

  • Day 8

    Down to Kochi, depart.

Where you stay

A heritage hotel in Fort Kochi, a premium two-bedroom houseboat with crew, a lake-facing resort at Kumarakom with a lift, and a plantation stay at Thekkady.

Who it’s for

Multi-generational families and anyone whose ideal holiday involves a veranda. The walking is short and flat everywhere. Not for adrenaline-seekers, the most exciting thing that happens is a kingfisher.
