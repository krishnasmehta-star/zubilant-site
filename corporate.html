Types of corporate tours

Four kinds of trip. One standard.

Each is built differently because each is for something different. Tell us which one you need and the plan changes shape around it.

01

Outbound training program

Structured learning away from the desk. Facilitated activities, problem-solving in the field and debriefs that carry back to work. We handle the venue, the safety cover and the schedule so the facilitator can run the room.

Offsite

Two to four days for planning, strategy or simply getting the team in one place. A working room that actually works, meals that keep to time, and evenings with something worth doing. Leadership, department or whole-company.

Incentive tour

The reward for a target met. These trips are judged on how they feel, so the hotels are a grade up, the pacing is generous, and the moments people will photograph are planned rather than hoped for. India or abroad.

Workation

A longer stay, a week or more, where the team works from somewhere better than the office. Reliable connectivity and desks checked in person, quiet hours respected, and the place itself waiting when the laptops close.

How we work

How an offsite gets built.

Three steps. A person designs it, not a booking engine, and the same person is on the phone throughout.

01

Enquire

Headcount, rough dates, and what the trip is for: reward, planning, training or a working week away. Those answers change everything downstream, so we ask them first and budget second.

02

We customise

A costed plan with the logistics visible: coach capacities, drive times, room splits, dietary counts, the meeting room’s actual dimensions, and the wet-weather alternative for anything outdoors.

03

On-ground support

A Zubilant coordinator travels with the group, from the first pick-up to the last drop. Changes on the day are handled on the day, and your HR or admin lead gets one number to call.
