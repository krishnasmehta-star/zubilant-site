How we work

How an offsite gets built.

Three steps. A person designs it, not a booking engine, and the same person is on the phone throughout.

01

Enquire

Headcount, rough dates, and what the trip is for: reward, planning, training or a working week away. Those answers change everything downstream, so we ask them first and budget second.

02

We customise

A costed plan with the logistics visible: coach capacities, drive times, room splits, dietary counts, the meeting room’s actual dimensions, and the wet-weather alternative for anything outdoors.

03

On-ground support

A Zubilant coordinator travels with the group, from the first pick-up to the last drop. Changes on the day are handled on the day, and your HR or admin lead gets one number to call.

The shape of a three-day offsite

A rhythm that works. Yours gets built around your agenda.

Not a schedule, a shape. Work in the mornings when people are sharp, something worth remembering in the evenings, and no 6am coach on the last day.

Day 1Arrive and settleLate-morning arrival · lunch together · one opening session · an easy evening
Day 2The working dayTwo sessions before lunch · one after · the activity everyone photographs · dinner out
Day 3Close and go homeA short wrap-up · free time · afternoon departure, not a dawn one
ThroughoutOne coordinatorRooms, meals, transfers and the wet-weather plan, handled without a WhatsApp group of 40
