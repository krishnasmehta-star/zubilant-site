Bali, Indonesia · 6 nights · Gentle

Bali Beyond the Pool Villa

Six nights across Ubud's rice terraces, the quiet Sidemen valley and the Uluwatu cliffs, the Bali underneath the Instagram version, on a visa stamped at the airport.

The journey

Bali has a problem, and the problem is that most visitors see about eight per cent of it, a villa in Seminyak, a swing photo, a beach club bill. The island underneath is one of the most distinctive cultures in Asia: a Hindu island in the world's largest Muslim country, where every morning begins with offerings of flowers and rice on every doorstep. You can watch that Bali or you can miss it entirely from a sun lounger, and most itineraries are engineered to miss it.

We split the six nights three ways, and the middle third is the one nobody sells: Sidemen, a river valley under Mount Agung an hour east of Ubud, all rice terraces and no traffic, where our travellers routinely report the best two days of the trip. Ubud gives you the temples, the Tegalalang terraces at 7 a.m. before the swings open, and a proper legong dance performance. Uluwatu at the end gives you the clifftop temple at sunset with the kecak fire dance, a hundred men chanting as the sun drops into the ocean, and a last beach day that feels earned.

For Indian travellers the mechanics are painless: visa on arrival, direct flights from several metros, and the Balinese warmth towards Indian guests is genuine, they will talk to you about the Ramayana, because it is their story too. Best in the April-to-October dry season; we are honest that January brings rain most afternoons.

Day by day

  • Day 1

    Arrive Denpasar, visa on arrival, transfer up to Ubud. Evening walk through the town and dinner overlooking a paddy. No alarm set.

  • Day 2

    Ubud, Tegalalang rice terraces at 7 a.m., a family temple visit with offerings explained, the Sacred Monkey Forest (hold your sunglasses), legong dance in the evening.

  • Day 3

    Ubud to Sidemen via Tirta Empul water temple, where you may join the purification ritual or simply watch. Afternoon arrival into the valley; the pace drops on purpose.

  • Day 4

    Sidemen, morning walk through rice terraces with a local guide, weaving workshop in the village, afternoon free with Mount Agung on the horizon.

  • Day 5

    Sidemen to Uluwatu, stopping at a coffee plantation. Uluwatu temple at golden hour and the kecak fire dance at sunset.

  • Day 6

    Free beach day, Melasti or Padang Padang, or the hotel pool. Farewell seafood dinner on Jimbaran beach, grilled at your table with your feet in the sand.

  • Day 7

    Transfer to the airport, depart.

Where you stay

Small boutique properties, not chain resorts, a garden hotel in the Ubud paddies, a valley lodge in Sidemen where the view from breakfast is rice terraces and volcano, and a clifftop-area hotel near Uluwatu with a proper pool. Character over stars, and every one a place we have used for years.

Who it’s for

Honeymooners who want more than a villa gate, and families with children old enough to sit through a temple visit, the pace is gentle and the drives short. Not for nightlife seekers; we deliberately skip Seminyak and Canggu, and Sidemen goes quiet by nine.
